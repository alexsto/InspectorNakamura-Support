Frequently asked questions

How do I win?
Work out who killed Lady Vivienne Carfax, with which weapon, and why. Interrogate the six guests, collect evidence around the camp, and when you're sure, press ACCUSE! and name the murderer, the weapon and the motive.
How long do I have?
Seven days, each split into five periods (meals and activities). Time moves on as you travel around the camp, and searches and weapon analyses in your tent are limited, so use them wisely.
What happens if I accuse the wrong person?
The investigation continues and time moves on. Accuse too many innocent guests and the case is lost.
Does the tone of my questions matter?
Yes. Each guest responds best to a friendly, suggestive or aggressive approach, and their trust meter shows how they feel about you. Guests who trust you share more; push the wrong way and they may refuse to answer.
Is the mystery the same every time?
No. Every new game generates a new murderer, weapon, motive, alibis and rumors, so each case is different.
Where are my case notes?
Tap the robot, Dex, in the top corner. Suspect Profiles records every alibi, witness, relationship and rumor you've uncovered.
Does the game need an internet connection or an account?
No. The game runs entirely on your device, with no account, ads, tracking or in-app purchases.
