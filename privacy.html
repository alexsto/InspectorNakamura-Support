None. The Game does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information or usage data. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs that collect data, no accounts and no in-app purchases. The Game does not connect to the internet.

The Game saves a small number of preferences (for example, audio volume and whether to skip the instructions) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the Game.
